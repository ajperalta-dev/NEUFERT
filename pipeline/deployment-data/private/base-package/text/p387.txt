Rest room
Provides relaxation between or after visits to the sauna. It should
be well ventilated and have visual contact with the outside and a
low noise level. The design and furnishing should be suitable for
rest and relaxation.
Solarium: an area of approx. 0.80 x 2.00 m is required per lying
place. The side aisle width is 0.40 m.
