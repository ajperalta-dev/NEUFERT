Climate in Animal Housing
In addition to their own characteristics, fodder and behaviour, the
climate in their housing has the most decisive influence on the performance and health of the animals. 'Climate' includes the factors temperature, humidity, air movement, air composition, light, ventilation,
window area, building volume, orientation of building and its thermal
insulation. The air intake speed should be between 2.0 and 5.0 m/s
according to the width of the building. Ventilation systems are divided
