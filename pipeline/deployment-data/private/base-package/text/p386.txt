Sauna/Wellness

Spa is the general term for health and wellness establishments,

which should generally include: sauna facility, massage and
solarium, relaxation, fitness and condition training (including
swimming -7 Indoor and outdoor swimming pools).
A commercially operated sauna (size III-IV -7 E)) will include:
Changing room, shower room with washing facilities, sauna
cabins, rest/relaxation room and subsidiary rooms (staff room,

reception, cash desk, sanitary facilities for visitors and staff).
In public saunas, separate rooms are provided for changing,
preliminary washing and toilets; for staff and visitors' toilets, the
relevant building regulations apply. Access to swimming areas,
food/drink providers and fitness areas is increasingly being offered
in spa and well ness establishments.
