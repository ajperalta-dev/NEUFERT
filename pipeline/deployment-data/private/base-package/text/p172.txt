Details and fitting out
Wardrobes and linen cupboards are an essential part of fitting out
a home. They serve to store (larger) items of clothing, linen, shoes
and suitcases, and are normally situated in the bedroom.

The essential elements of a wardrobe are a drawer unit, a
hanging rail and additional shelves. It can be a free-standing
wardrobe -7 0. a built-in wardrobe (wall cupboard, single or
double wardrobe constructions) -7 f)- 0 or in the form of a walkin wardrobe or dressing room -7 0
