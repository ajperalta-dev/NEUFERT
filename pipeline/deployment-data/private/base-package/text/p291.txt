Basic division: storerooms, production rooms, sales rooms, building services rooms, administration and business rooms, social
and subsidiary rooms ~ 0. Work processes in or between the

Basic types of storage
Raw material store: grains, sugar, salt, baking agents, dry goods in
sacks, flour in silos or sacks.
Ingredients room: fruit, toppings, dry fruit, fats, eggs.
Packaging store: space requirement for containers {shelving,
racks, cupboards), stacking, counters. Space for traffic (passages).
Minimum area for stores 15 m2 ; approx. 8-1 0 m2 per employee
for all stores.
Short routes between stores and work areas.
Separation of workrooms for bakery and pastries
Bakery requires warm and humid room climate; pastry room
should be cooler. Bakery has following areas: dough preparation,
dough processing, baking, storage of finished products. Pastry
room: cold area - cream, creme, chocolate, fruit; warm area ready mixes, kitchen, fine pastries.
Workroom area is sum of:
Space required for equipment, handling and processing, intermediate storage (trolleys) and side counters. Space for traffic (passages); lost space.
