The following tables show the plot size required according to production capacity and type of operation, based on investigations
by Herms and Hillendahl. Various plot areas can be reduced, e.g.
through the installation of a tower silo instead of a silage heap,
upstairs instead of downstairs feed rooms, slurry storage under
the slatted floor instead of in outdoor containers, building up to
boundaries etc.
The tables of plot size -7 0 - 8 do not take into account all the
space required for housing machines and workshops or for the
residential area, as these do not have to be in the farmyard.
